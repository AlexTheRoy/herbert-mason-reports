Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020-2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), table-based summary file, tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage1,444 populated ZIPs: every Illinois ZIP (600 to 629) and Northwest Indiana (463 and 464). PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile. Median income is published for 1,391 ZIPs and median home value for 1,390. 60 of your Home leads used a ZIP outside the study area and are left out of the ZIP tables.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps median income at “$250,000 or more” (3 ZIPs, shown at the cap). No ZIP reaches the “$2,000,000 or more” cap on home value.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file). Cook County is split into the City of Chicago (606 ZIPs), the South & Southwest Suburbs (ZIP centers south of about 75th Street) and the North & West Suburbs; counties outside the Chicago area are grouped into northern, central and southern Illinois. The spreadsheet's city column is left blank: no city file was used; the area names in section 04 are the city named most often on your lead records.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesMason-IL-ZIP-Data-10-29-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the severe weather tier from the companion report and your Home lead and sold counts by ZIP.
